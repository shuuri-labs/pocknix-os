# PATCHES.md — patch ledger (what pocknix-os carries, on which base, and why)

Records only what this repo controls: the pinned base, the patch series, where each
patch came from, what was dropped and why. Never restate another project's state here
(what armada or ROCKNIX pin or carry, who is ahead): it goes stale the day they move and
nobody re-checks. Read theirs live: armada = `packages/kernel/PATCHES.md` +
`patches/series` in armada-os/armada; ROCKNIX = the `vendor/rocknix-<soc>` sync
(`kernel/<soc>/kernel.conf` pins the commit). Rebase procedure: pocknix-notes
dev/patch-rebase.md.
